From JAMB result to graduation. Live it.

Campus Life is a free game about Nigerian university life. Get your result, win admission, survive clearance, run your hostel, pass your exams and walk at convocation. Play on your phone, right now.

Free. No ads. No signup. No download.

How it works

  1. Choose your story

    Pick your name, your background and your home town, then your two JAMB choices of university and course.

  2. Wait for the result

    Check your JAMB score and the admission list. Get admitted, get a different course, change school or retake.

  3. Arrive and settle in

    Travel to campus, do clearance, find your hostel and learn your way around.

  4. Study, earn, belong

    Balance lectures, money, food, sleep, health and friends. Pass your exams year after year.

  5. Graduate

    Reach convocation, see your story and share your graduation card.

What is inside

Three campuses

Play at a game version of UNILAG, UI or OAU, each with its own buildings and feel.

Real student problems

Clearance queues, hostel bills, light going off, strikes, and the pressure before exam day.

Courses and exams

Named courses and quiz-style exams from 100 level to 400 level in nine departments.

Life beyond lectures

Cook, work small jobs, join clubs, make friends, play football and look after your health.

Made for phones

Touch controls, big buttons, works in portrait and landscape, and keeps playing offline after the first visit.

Your progress is yours

Saves stay on your device, with automatic backup and a save code to move to another phone.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no ads, no payments and no hidden purchases.

Do I need an account or an app?

No. Open the link in your phone browser and play. Your progress is saved on your device.

Is this connected to UNILAG, UI, OAU or JAMB?

No. Campus Life is an independent, fan-made game. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any university, JAMB or any other institution. Their names are used only to describe the places and events the game is inspired by. The game uses no logos or crests. Buildings, people, prices, scores and events are fictional or simplified, and some admission cut-offs are game estimates, so do not use the game for real admission decisions.

What happens to my data?

Your save stays in your browser. Anonymous usage data is sent only if you switch it on in the settings. Read the privacy policy for details.

Will it work on my phone?

It is built for phones, including older Android phones, and has a Low graphics setting. If something is wrong, use the feedback button in the game.

Does the game end?

Yes, at graduation. If you fall short, the game has an ending for that too.
