Privacy policy

The short version

  • There are no accounts. You do not give us your name, email or phone number to play.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your own device, in your browser.
  • We collect nothing about how you play unless you switch on anonymous usage data yourself.
  • There are no ads, no advertising trackers and no sale of data.

What is stored on your device

To remember your game, Campus Life saves information in your browser's local storage: your character name (any name you type), your game progress, money, grades, friendships and settings. It also keeps a recent backup of your save and a short list of recent error messages to help with support. This information stays on your device. Clearing your browser data, or using a private window, removes it. You can export a copy of your save from the phone's Settings.

AI chat with characters (only if switched on for the game)

If the game offers "Ask something" in conversations, the question you type is sent through our server to an AI provider so the character can reply. Along with it we send your in-game name, your course and university, the day and time in the game, and facts such as your timetable. We do not send your real identity, and we do not store what you type. Do not type personal information such as phone numbers, addresses or passwords. You can switch AI chat off in the phone's Settings, and the characters will use their normal lines.

Optional anonymous usage data

In the phone's Settings you can choose to share anonymous usage data. It is off by default. If you switch it on, the game may send simple events such as "reached the first exam" or "a crash happened", with no name, no save contents and no text you typed. Switch it off at any time to stop.

Feedback

The Send feedback option can copy a short report to your clipboard: the game version, your device type and screen size, roughly where you are in the game and any recent error messages. It contains no name and no money amounts. Nothing is sent unless you choose to paste or send it. If a feedback form or email address is provided, what you write there goes to that form or inbox.

The website host

Like any website, the company that hosts Campus Life may keep ordinary server logs (for example your IP address and the time you visited) for security and to run the service. We do not use these logs to identify players.

Children

Campus Life is intended for players aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. If you are a parent or guardian and have a concern, contact us using the details below.

Your rights

Because the game does not collect personal information about you, there is normally nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your save belongs to you and is on your device. If you believe we hold personal information about you, contact us and we will respond. This policy is written with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 in mind.

Changes

If we add features that need more data (such as accounts or cloud save), we will update this policy first and ask for your consent where needed.
