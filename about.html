What this is

Campus Life is a free, independent, fan-made browser game about Nigerian student life: waiting for a JAMB result, getting admission, clearance, hostel life, lectures, exams, friendships and graduation. It is made for fun and for entertainment.

Not affiliated with anyone

Campus Life is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any university, school, exam board or institution that is mentioned in it, including the University of Lagos (UNILAG), the University of Ibadan (UI), Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU), JAMB, the NUC or any other body. Their names appear only to describe the settings the game is inspired by. The game uses no logos, crests or official images.

It is fiction

  • The buildings, layouts, people, events, prices, scores and exams in the game are fictional or simplified. They are not accurate maps or records of any real campus or person.
  • The admission cut-off marks for UNILAG are based on that university's publicly released 2025/2026 list. The cut-offs for UI and OAU are game estimates. Do not use any of it for real admission decisions.
  • The quiz questions are for entertainment and light revision only. They are not an official syllabus and are not a substitute for real study or real exam preparation.
  • Sickness, treatment and money in the game are for gameplay and are not medical or financial advice.

Credits
