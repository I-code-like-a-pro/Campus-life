# Marketing kit

Free, ad-free, single-player, in the browser, no download. Everything here is built from moments that exist in the game today. No statistics are claimed. Check anything about other games or real institutions yourself before posting; nothing below has been verified against outside sources.

**Always include (short form):** "Fan-made game. Not affiliated with UNILAG, UI, OAU, JAMB or any institution." Use no logos or crests in any post image.

## One-line hooks (pick one and use it everywhere)
1. From JAMB result to graduation. Live it, free, on your phone.
2. Can you survive 100 level? Free in your browser.
3. Clearance queue. NEPA. Exam day. The whole story, without the real stress.
4. Your admission list is out. Did you get your course?
5. Four years of campus life in a game you can play on your data plan.
6. Pick your university. Pick your course. Try not to carry over.

Pick for tone: 1 is the clearest, 2 and 6 are the most shareable, 3 is the most relatable.

## Ten post ideas from real game moments
Record a screen capture on a phone (vertical), 10 to 25 seconds, with captions. Do not show private data.

1. **JAMB result day.** Capture the result screen reveal. Caption: "Result is out. Press to check." Ask: "What score did you get? Mine in the game: ___."
2. **The admission list.** Show the cyber cafe check and the outcome (admitted, different course, retake). Caption: "Admitted, or are they sending you to a different course?"
3. **The journey to campus.** Show the bus or flight with a road event. Caption: "Kano to Lagos by road is a journey in itself." (The game computes trip length by distance.)
4. **Clearance queue.** Walk the queue and the offices. Caption: "Clearance: tell us you survived without crying."
5. **NEPA.** Capture the light going off during study or phone charging. Caption: "NEPA took light at the worst time. Again." Ask people to share their worst NEPA moment.
6. **Hostel life.** Cooking, bills and room decorations. Caption: "Foodstuff finished before the month did."
7. **Exam day.** Show a timed question and the result. Caption: "Exam day. Did you read, or did you hope?" (Add the real pass mark or timer only after checking the build you ship.)
8. **Football match.** Short 3v3 clip with a goal. Caption: "Matchday on the faculty pitch. Kick button on the left thumb."
9. **ASUU strike announcement.** Show the evening announcement. Caption: "Lectures suspended. Do you read ahead or go home?"
10. **Graduation share card.** Show the convocation and the generated card with the final CGPA. Caption: "I graduated with ___. Post your share card." This is the main viral loop: ask players to post theirs.

Format notes: TikTok and Instagram Reels vertical with captions burnt in; X posts with a clip under the platform limit plus a one-line hook; always end with the link and "free, no signup".

## Three-week launch calendar
Dates assume the plan in [LAUNCH_PLAN.md](LAUNCH_PLAN.md) (public launch around 21 November). Move them with the launch.

**Week 1 (teaser, 1 to 7 Nov)**
- Mon: set up handles; pin a post with the hook and the disclaimer.
- Tue to Sun: post ideas 1, 5 and 4 (one per two days). Ask followers which university they want first.
- Owner: build the ambassador list; send the outreach template to 10 people.

**Week 2 (closed beta, 8 to 14 Nov)**
- Share the beta link with ambassadors and class groups only.
- Post ideas 2, 6, 7 and 8. Collect testimonials with permission.
- Capture real screenshots and a 20 to 30 second trailer; update the landing page.
- Send the press blurb to 5 to 10 relevant writers and campus pages (verify their contact details yourself).

**Week 3 (launch, 15 to 21 Nov)**
- Mon to Wed: countdown posts (ideas 3 and 9).
- Launch day: trailer, hook, link, disclaimer; ask ambassadors to post the same day; reply to every comment in the first 3 hours.
- Days after launch: idea 10 daily, repost the best share cards (with permission), thank early players, fix and announce the top bug.

Players choose a home town and a course at UNILAG, UI or OAU, manage money, health, study time and social life, and finish with a shareable graduation card. It runs on a phone with no download or signup, and saves progress on the device. Campus Life is an independent fan-made project and is not affiliated with any university or exam board. ## What to measure (all opt-in or from the host, no personal data)
- Landing page visits and the click rate on "Play now" (host analytics, if you enable them).
- Players who reach the first campus day (opt-in event), and how many reach each stage: result, admission, clearance, first exam, end of year 1, graduation.
- Median session length and number of returning players on day 2 and day 7.
- Crash-free sessions and top errors from the opt-in telemetry stub.
- Feedback form volume, the top 5 themes, and the share of respondents who would recommend it.
- Share cards posted (search the hashtag manually; this is approximate).
- Per-channel referral (use a different link tag per channel, for example `?src=tiktok`; check the host handles it).
Set targets after the beta, once you know the real numbers. Because telemetry is opt-in, treat counts as a lower bound.
