# UNILAG structure: research summary

Researched 2026-11-06. Data: `data/unilag/facts.json` (62 facts). Review sheet: `docs/review/unilag-structure.md`.

## Important limitation

The official site (unilag.edu.ng) could not be fetched directly: every request failed with a DNS error from the research environment. Official pages were read only through web-search result summaries, which sometimes cut lists short or garble them. Nothing here is a verbatim copy of an official page, and every fact is `verified: false`.

## What was found

- Identity: name, short name, founding year (1962), motto ("In deed and in truth"), main campus at Akoka plus Idi-Araba (College of Medicine) and a Yaba site.
- Faculties: UNILAG moved from 12 to 19 faculties, with the official list effective 1 August 2025. The 19 names were consistent across two official-page summaries. Departments were found for most faculties.
- Residence: about 12 hall names from official 2025 notices, a "16 undergraduate halls" figure from an official guidelines PDF, and the 2025/26 balloting process (open to 200-500 level students).
- Calendar: two semesters, 14 teaching weeks each and about three weeks of exams, per the 2025/26 calendar (which was revised several times).
- Degree classes: First Class 4.50-5.00 down to Pass 1.00-1.49 from a UNILAG handbook.
- Admissions: UTME plus Post-UTME (200 minimum to be eligible in 2025/26) and Direct Entry, at a high level.
- Landmarks and traditions: library, chapel, mosque, main auditorium, matriculation and convocation.

## Biggest gaps

- Faculty department lists that are probably partial: Education, Environmental Sciences, Life Sciences, Clinical Sciences, Communication and Media Studies.
- Hall gender split: only one hall (Mariere, male) is confirmed officially. The rest come from student guides that disagree.
- Letter-grade table with mark ranges, credit unit rules and course code prefixes (CSC, MTH and so on): no UNILAG source seen, so values are null or low confidence.
- Fees (undergraduate tuition and hall fees): null, no official schedule found.
- Per-course cut-off marks: not recorded (official notice table was unreadable).
- Student union structure, famous student events, nicknames, faculty colours: not found or not confirmed.

## Weakest facts

1. Engineering departments: sources disagree (8, 9 or 10 departments).
2. Hall male/female split (low): unofficial, inconsistent.
3. Grade points per letter (low): unofficial 5-point scale only.
4. "New faculties" list: the summary named 8 although 19 minus 12 is 7, so one may be a renamed faculty.
5. Education and Environmental Sciences departments: known partial lists.

Also note that a Dec 2025 blog (studentsdash.com) lists 19 completely different faculties (Aviation, Film and so on). It contradicts the official list and was not used.
