# Campus Life: full student-life scope (JAMB to graduation)

This is the complete journey a player lives, stage by stage. Each system is tagged:

- ✅ built and working now
- 🟡 partly built (what is missing is noted)
- ⬜ not built yet
- 🎯 recommended for the launch (next 4 weeks)
- 🔮 after launch

Game time: 1 game day is about 10 real minutes. A semester is 6 days plus a Sunday break. A session is 100L to 400L (500L for Medicine/Engineering is a post-launch option).

## Recommended 4-week plan to a launch-ready scope

**Week 1 (done):** performance, save safety, legal pages, balance, QA checklist.

**Week 2: content and the full arc**
1. Matriculation, orientation week, clearance hiccups.
2. 200L and 300L course lists; 40+ questions per department, 20 GST.
3. Degree class, probation and withdrawal.
4. Final-year project, graduation ceremony, final summary screen.

**Week 3: depth and polish**
1. SIWES, scholarships, SUG election, religious/cultural events, water shortage.
2. Landmark badges, guided first day.
3. Share cards.
4. Real-phone testing and closed beta (about 10 people).

**Week 4: ship**
1. Fix beta feedback, balance pass.
2. Real hosting (Vercel), domain, landing page, feedback form.
3. Launch-week social posts (see the marketing notes in chat).

## Definition of done for launch
A new player can finish the whole story in one or two evenings of play (about 60 to 90 real minutes for the fast route) with no crashes: JAMB, admission, clearance, matriculation, at least two semesters, a graduation or an honest failure ending, and a final summary they want to share.
